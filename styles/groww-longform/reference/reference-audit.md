## Frame-type taxonomy (8 types, as used across all three audits)

1. **Creator** — talking head on real wall, bare or with a small text overlay (serif labels, typewriter captions).
2. **Creator + data panel** — creator reframed left/behind, lower-third or side panel carrying real data (tables, timelines, callouts, subscribe unit in one audit's coding).
3. **Creator + text + illustration** — creator with built mixed serif/sans text plus complementing illustrations (rotating gold coins, football art). Hook/question/agenda treatment.
4. **Text-only card** — two families sharing one type: lavender topic cards (purple serif-italic title, dotted grid, rotating gold coins) and white legal/disclaimer pages.
5. **Full-screen brand MG** — lavender-grid motion graphics: data tables, pies, bars, fund circles, concept scenes, screener screenshots.
6. **Full-screen MG + resolving text** — a serif takeaway title lands on the running MG exactly on the VO's conclusion clause; also the recap card and the endscreen (and, once in C, an off-brand stock template).
7. **AI/CGI b-roll** — generated cutaways.
8. **Real-footage b-roll** — stock/filmed cutaways.

Taxonomy notes: bare talking-head has no plainer bucket than 1; legal cards have no bucket other than 4 (do NOT template them as topic cards); types 7 and 8 wear an identical dust treatment (4-side black box + grain/scanline) so they are interchangeable at the edit level — 8 when the image is filmable, 7 when it is not.

## FINDINGS — devices the instruction sheet's transition table does not list

The sheet's in-scope kinds: zoom-in/out, light-leak, brand-transition, straight cut onto topic card, element entrances. Everything below is observed in the references but absent from that table, ranked by frequency:

1. **Plain hard cut (not onto a topic card)** — the majority device overall (~75 in C alone, 47 in B, ~14 in A). Used for: creator↔b-roll, b-roll↔b-roll, table→table swaps inside a data run, graphics arriving fully formed, and the endscreen→legal chain. C proves an entire 13-minute video can run on hard cuts + element entrances alone.
2. **No-cut overlay swap** (≈30 pooled: 18 in C, ~8 in B, 2 in A) — text/coin overlays, lower-third data panels, inline serif mini-labels and the subscribe unit pop on/off over a continuous creator take. Frame-type changes 1↔2 and 1↔3 are overlay events, not cuts.
3. **Dissolve / cross-fade** (~15: 13 in A, 2 in B) — the standard device for same-layout Nippon↔Bandhan table swaps and table→topic-card moves. Invisible to cut detection.
4. **Whip-cut** (motion-blurred hard cut, ~15 in A only) — functionally the zoom family under heavy blur; used creator↔b-roll and card→creator+panel. Includes the crash-zoom with radial blur (A 466.35) and whip-through-blank-frame (A 637.12).
5. **In-place slot swap / element morph on a continuous background** (~15: 7 in A, ~6 in B, 2 in C) — panel content re-types, table→pie, card→chart, card→table build morph, text→card morph. The background never cuts; 5→6 title resolves ALWAYS happen this way.
6. **Straight hard cut onto a topic card whose elements build after** (4 flagged in B; 7 in C; 2 in A) — bg cuts in first, serif title animates 0.1–1 s later. (The sheet has "straight cut onto topic card" but not this build-after pattern per one audit's reading — treat as the canonical card entry.)
7. **Masked cuts** — light-streak pass, speed-streak, defocus-landing that racks into focus, scale-down exit + cut (A ×4), 1-frame dip-to-black (B 433.5).
8. **Jump cuts / reframes within a continuous talking-head** (C ×4) — repositioning the creator to make room for a text panel.
9. **Card-to-card cut with no creator beat** (C 550.52, unique) — topic card straight into a data table.
10. **Reversed brand-wipe direction** (B 155.9, R→L; A 305.1 single-slab R→L variant).
