Daily vs Weekly vs Monthly SIP!

Daily SIP karun, Weekly, ya fir Monthly?[a] Konsi frequency se sabse jyada returns milenge? 🤔[b] Aao dekhte hain data kya kehta hai!

Socho aapne August 1996 se June 2026 tak total ₹109.08 Lakhs invest kiye.

Agar Daily ₹1,000 ki SIP ki, toh valuation banti hai ₹12.33 Crore!

Agar Weekly ₹6,997 ki, toh bhi ₹12.33 Crore!

Aur agar Monthly ₹30,384 invest kiye... tab bhi valuation mili ₹12.45 Crore!

Teeno frequencies ka XIRR exactly same nikla, 13.47%!


Source: Whiteoak Capital[c]

Matlab long term mein frequency se koi farak nahi padta! Daily, weekly ya monthly, returns lagbhag same hi hote hain.[d]

Toh lesson kya hai? Frequency par mat uljho, discipline aur long-term investing par focus karo!
